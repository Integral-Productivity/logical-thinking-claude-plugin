- **Cause insufficiency.** Enough drivers work on peak days [enough-drivers]: Hiring alone is not enough, because seasonal drivers must also be available to hire and onboarded before the peak weeks start.
- **Cause insufficiency.** Deliveries arrive on time on peak days [on-time]: Enough drivers alone is not enough, because there must also be enough vehicles and dispatch capacity for the extra drivers.
- **Possible negative branch.** Hire seasonal drivers for peak weeks [hire]: New seasonal drivers who don't know the routes may drive slower routes and misdeliver more, which can keep deliveries late or create new errors.
