- **Entity existence.** Seasonal drivers learn the routes before peak weeks [know-routes]: drivers hired only for peak weeks will not know the routes beforehand unless an injection such as paid pre-peak route training makes that happen.
- **Cause insufficiency.** Enough drivers work on peak days [enough-drivers]: hiring alone is not enough; enough seasonal drivers must be available to hire during the peak labor market, and those hired must show up on peak days.
- **Possible negative branch.** Hire seasonal drivers for peak weeks [hire]: recruiting, onboarding and training seasonal drivers raises labor costs and takes experienced staff's time just before the peak, which the tree does not show.
