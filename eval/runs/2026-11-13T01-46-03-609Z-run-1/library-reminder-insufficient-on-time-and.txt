- **Cause insufficiency.** More books come back on time [on-time]: knowing the due date is not enough on its own; borrowers must also be able and willing to get to the library before that date.
- **Possible negative branch.** Some borrowers turn off library messages [opt-out]: borrowers who turn off all library messages also stop getting reminders, overdue notices and hold alerts, so for them late returns could rise above where they were before.
