- **Cause insufficiency.** The kitchen is cleaned every day [clean]: knowing their day and being home is not enough; each person must also be willing to clean, and the rota must assign every day of the week to someone.
- **Cause insufficiency.** People argue over swapped cleaning days [swaps]: posting a rota alone does not cause arguments; people must also need to swap days, for example because they are not home on their day, and the swaps must go unrecorded or be disputed.
- **Cause insufficiency.** Each person knows their cleaning day [know-day]: posting the rota is not enough; each person must also see and read it.
