- **Cause insufficiency.** The kitchen is cleaned every day [clean]: knowing their day and being home is not enough; every day must also have someone assigned on the rota, and each person must actually be willing to clean.
- **Possible negative branch.** Post a cleaning rota in the shared kitchen [rota]: with a fixed rota, people may stop tidying up after themselves on days that are not theirs, so the mess grows between cleanings.
