- **Cause insufficiency.** Each person knows their cleaning day [know-day]: posting the rota is not enough on its own; people must also read the rota where it hangs.
- **Cause insufficiency.** The kitchen is cleaned every day [clean]: knowing the day, having supplies and being home still need people who are willing to do their share, and a rota that assigns someone to every day.
- **Cause insufficiency.** People argue over swapped cleaning days [swaps]: a posted rota alone does not cause arguments; people must also need to swap days and disagree about how swaps are made or repaid.
