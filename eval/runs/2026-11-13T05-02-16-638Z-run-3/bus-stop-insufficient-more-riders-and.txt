- **Cause insufficiency.** More pupils ride the bus [more-riders]: living near a stop is not enough on its own, because the newly nearby pupils must also have been kept off the bus by distance and must have a seat available.
- **Cause insufficiency.** Pupils at the first stop spend longer on the bus [longer-ride]: a longer route lengthens their ride only if the second stop lies between the first stop and the school.
- **Possible negative branch.** More pupils ride the bus [more-riders]: the tree does not draw the foreseeable effect that more riders can overcrowd the bus or exceed its seating capacity.
- **Possible negative branch.** The route takes longer [longer-route]: the tree does not draw the foreseeable effect that pupils reach school later or the first pickup must move earlier.
