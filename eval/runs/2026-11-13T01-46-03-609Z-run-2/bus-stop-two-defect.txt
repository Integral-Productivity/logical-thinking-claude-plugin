- **Cause insufficiency.** More pupils ride the bus [more-riders]: living near a stop is not enough on its own; the bus must also have spare seats and a timetable that suits those pupils.
- **Cause insufficiency.** More pupils live near a stop [near-stop]: adding a stop is not enough; it must also be placed where pupils who are not already near a stop live.
- **Possible negative branch.** Add a second stop on the school bus route [second-stop]: an extra stop lengthens the route, so existing riders may face longer journeys or late arrival at school.
