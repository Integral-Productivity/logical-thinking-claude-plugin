- **Cause insufficiency.** The baker stays up late the night before [late-night]: baking the night before only keeps the baker up late if the baking also starts late or takes a long time.
- **Cause insufficiency.** The stall sells muffins [sells]: ready muffins and visitors are not enough on their own, because the visitors must also want the muffins at the price offered.
- **Possible negative branch.** Bake a batch of muffins the night before [bake]: muffins baked the night before may be less fresh by sale time, which the tree does not draw and which could reduce sales.
