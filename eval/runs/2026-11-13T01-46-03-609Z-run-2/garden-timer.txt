- **Cause insufficiency.** Beds are watered every morning [watered]: the timer alone does not water the beds; the water supply must also be running each morning, so the supply belongs in an AND group with the timer here.
- **Causality existence.** Plants get enough water [enough-water]: the running supply adds nothing once the beds are already watered, so its direct arrow to this effect is misplaced.
- **Possible negative branch.** The timer waters the beds on rainy days too [rain-watering]: watering on top of rain can waterlog the beds and harm the plants, and the tree does not draw that effect.
