- **Cause insufficiency.** More books come back on time [on-time]: knowing the due date is not enough; the borrower must also be able and willing to return the book within the two days, for example by having finished it and being able to get to the library.
- **Cause insufficiency.** Some borrowers turn off library messages [opt-out]: annoyance alone is not enough; the library must also offer a setting that lets borrowers turn messages off.
- **Additional cause.** Borrowers know the due date in advance [know-date]: borrowers may already know the due date from the checkout receipt or their account, so the reminder may not be what makes them know it.
