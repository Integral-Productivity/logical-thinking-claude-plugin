- **Cause insufficiency.** Beds are watered every morning [watered]: installing a timer is not enough; the timer must also be set to a morning schedule and the water supply must be running then, so the supply precondition belongs in this AND group rather than at Plants get enough water.
- **Cause insufficiency.** Plants get enough water [enough-water]: daily watering and a running supply are not enough; the watering duration and flow must also match what the plants need, otherwise they may get too little.
- **Possible negative branch.** Install a timer on the garden taps [timer]: a fixed timer waters even after rain or in cool weather, which foreseeably leads to waterlogged beds and wasted water that the tree does not show.
