**Cause insufficiency.** More pupils ride the bus [more-riders]: living near a stop is not enough on its own; those pupils must also prefer the bus to how they get to school now, and the bus must have free seats for them.

**Possible negative branch.** The route takes longer [longer-route]: unless the departure time moves earlier, a longer route also means the bus reaches school later, so pupils may arrive late, and the tree does not draw this.
