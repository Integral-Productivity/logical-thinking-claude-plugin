- **Cause insufficiency.** Enough drivers work on peak days [enough-drivers]: hiring seasonal drivers for peak weeks only covers peak days if the peaks can be forecast to fall within the hired weeks, which "Orders arrive in peaks" does not establish.
- **Cause insufficiency.** Deliveries arrive on time on peak days [on-time]: enough drivers alone will not deliver on time unless there are also enough vehicles and loading capacity for the extra drivers.
- **Possible negative branch.** Hire seasonal drivers for peak weeks [hire]: new seasonal drivers who don't know the routes or procedures will likely make slower or wrong deliveries on the very peak days the injection targets, and the tree does not draw this effect.
