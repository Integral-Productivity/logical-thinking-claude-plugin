- **Cause insufficiency.** The main road loses parking spaces [parking]: painting a lane removes parking only if the lane takes the curbside strip where cars now park, and the tree does not state that.
- **Entity existence.** Drivers keep out of the marked lane [respect]: painted lanes with no physical barrier are often entered or blocked by parked and delivering vehicles, so this precondition is doubtful as a given.
- **Possible negative branch.** Paint a bike lane on the main road [paint]: if the lane is not taken from parking, it narrows the general traffic lanes, which can slow traffic and increase conflicts with turning vehicles; the tree does not show this.
