- **Cause insufficiency.** Drivers see the cyclist [seen]: a switched-on light after dark is not enough, because drivers must also be looking toward the cyclist and the light must be bright and unobstructed in their line of sight.
- **Additional cause.** Drivers see the cyclist [seen]: drivers can also see a cyclist after dark through street lighting, their own headlights on reflectors, or reflective clothing, even with no bike light on.
