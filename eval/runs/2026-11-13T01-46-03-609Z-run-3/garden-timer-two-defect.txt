- **Cause insufficiency.** Beds are watered every morning [watered]: a timer alone does not water the beds; it also needs hoses or drip lines that run from the taps to the beds, and a schedule set for the morning.
- **Cause insufficiency.** Plants get enough water [enough-water]: watering every morning gives enough water only if the duration or volume of each watering matches what the plants need.
