- **Cause insufficiency.** People argue over swapped cleaning days [swaps]: posting a rota alone does not cause arguments; people must also need to swap days and have no agreed way to arrange or record swaps.
- **Cause insufficiency.** The kitchen is cleaned every day [clean]: knowing the day, being home and having supplies are not enough; the person must also be willing to do their turn, and the rota must assign someone to every day.
- **Possible negative branch.** Post a cleaning rota in the shared kitchen [rota]: a fixed rota invites a "not my day" attitude, so people leave their own mess for whoever is on duty, and the tree does not draw this.
