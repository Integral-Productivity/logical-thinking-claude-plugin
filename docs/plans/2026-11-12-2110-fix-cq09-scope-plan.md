# CQ-09 Scope - Plan

> **Moved.** On 2026-11-14 this plan moved to the logic-domains repository (`docs/plans/2026-11-12-2110-fix-cq09-scope-plan.md`), where the CQ-09 decision now lives. See [ADR 0014](../adr/0014-concrete-logic-domains-live-outside-the-plugin.md). This copy is kept for its history and is no longer updated. Issue #23 here now asks the general question.

## Goal Capsule

- **Objective:** A CQ-09 failure means a real "Field of Dreams" gap: a tree claims that provider work causes a customer action, but no release, launch or customer need is shown upstream. A customer action that the customer starts alone does not fail for that reason.
- **Product authority:** This plan covers the scope of CQ-09 (ADR 0001, decisions 8 and 15), tracked in issue #23. Fixing a particular tree is tree work, kept with that tree and not in this repository. The other competency questions are not active scope.
- **Means:** An explicit, reviewed exemption on the Customer Action box, plus a fix of the tree where the gap is real (R1-R6).
- **Open blockers:** The scope choice (Q1) needs the domain holder's decision. The recommended option is written into the Requirements as provisional.

## Product Contract

### Summary

Keep the no-bypass half of CQ-09 as it is. Keep the ancestor half for every Customer Action that adopts or uses something the provider made available. Let the author mark a Customer Action as customer-started, with a reason, so the Release and Launch part of the ancestor half does not apply to it. Every Customer Action still needs a Precondition upstream.

### Problem Frame

CQ-09 asks for a Release, a Launch and a Precondition among the ancestors of every Customer Action. In a trial on a real tree, 13 Customer Actions failed: 3 had no Release or Launch upstream, and 10 had none of the three. Some of these actions are things the customer does without any provider release, for example answering a survey or preparing for a meeting. For these, a required Release and Launch would add edges that carry no meaning, which is the problem decision 15 already fixed for direct inputs. Others may be real gaps. The check cannot tell the two apart today, so the domain holder put CQ-09 on hold.

### Requirements

**Check scope**

- R1. The no-bypass half of CQ-09 does not change: no path from an Initiative or Release reaches an outcome without passing a Customer Action.
- R2. Every Customer Action needs a Precondition (for example a Job or a Switch Trigger) among its ancestors. There is no exemption from this part.
- R3. A Customer Action needs a Release and a Launch among its ancestors, unless it carries `initiated_by=customer`.
- R4. A Customer Action with `initiated_by=customer` must give the reason in its box note. CQ-09 reports a missing reason as a failure.
- R5. CQ-09 lists every exempt Customer Action in its output, with its count, so the exemption cannot hide a gap. CLR review checks each one, in the same way it checks `skip_ok=yes` for CQ-06.

**Pilot tree**

- R6. In a tree that fails CQ-09, each failure is resolved in one of two ways: the author adds the missing upstream boxes or edges, or the author marks the action `initiated_by=customer` with a reason. CQ-09 then runs with no failures and is taken off hold.

### Acceptance Examples

- AE1 (covers R3). A Customer Action "Customer starts a trial" has a Launch and a Job upstream but no Release. CQ-09 fails it.
- AE2 (covers R2, R3, R4). A Customer Action "Customer answers a feedback survey" carries `initiated_by=customer`, the note says the survey is sent by email and needs no release, and a Job is upstream. CQ-09 passes it and lists it as exempt.
- AE3 (covers R2). The same survey action with `initiated_by=customer` but no Precondition upstream fails.
- AE4 (covers R4). A Customer Action with `initiated_by=customer` and an empty note fails.

### Key Decisions

- KD1. **Exempt by marking, not by inference.** The check does not guess which actions are customer-started. The author states it and CLR review checks it. This keeps the Field of Dreams test strict by default (ADR 0003, review on by default).
- KD2. **Precondition stays required for all actions.** A customer act with no need or trigger upstream is itself a gap in the tree, whoever starts the act.
- KD3. **This changes ADR 0001.** Decision 15 and the CQ-09 row in decision 8 need new wording, and the shared attribute set (decision 6) gains `initiated_by`. A superseding ADR or an amendment records it, and the tool layer's tree check follows (ADR 0001, Consequences).

### Scope Boundaries

- Deferred: a general "Offer" class that covers services, surveys and sessions as well as releases (option C in Outstanding Questions). It can come back if the exemption list grows long.
- Not in scope: other modelling questions on the trial tree, such as edge signs or directions between Customer Outcomes. They are tree work and do not change the scope of CQ-09.

### Outstanding Questions

- Q1 (Resolve Before Planning). Which scope does CQ-09 take? The options:
  - A. Keep CQ-09 as it is and fix all 13 in the tree, for example with one shared Release and Launch for the service that the customer actions depend on.
  - B. Exempt customer-started actions by marking (this plan's provisional choice, R3-R5).
  - C. Add an "Offer" concept: any provider act that makes something available (a release, a service, a survey) counts in place of Release and Launch.
  - D. Drop the ancestor half and keep only the no-bypass half.
- Q2 (Deferred to Planning). The attribute name and values (`initiated_by=customer|provider`). Planning confirms that the tool layer accepts the name and that it fits the short-value rule in ADR 0001 decision 6.
- Q3 (Deferred to Planning). In each failing tree, which failures are customer-started and which are real gaps. The tree's author decides per box when R6 is applied.
