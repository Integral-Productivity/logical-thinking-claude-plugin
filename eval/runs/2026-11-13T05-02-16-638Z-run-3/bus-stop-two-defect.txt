- **Cause insufficiency.** More pupils ride the bus [more-riders]: living near a stop is not enough on its own; the newly served pupils must also prefer the bus to how they travel now, such as being driven, walking or cycling.
- **Possible negative branch.** Add a second stop on the school bus route [second-stop]: an extra stop lengthens the run, so pupils already on the route have a longer ride or the bus reaches school later.
