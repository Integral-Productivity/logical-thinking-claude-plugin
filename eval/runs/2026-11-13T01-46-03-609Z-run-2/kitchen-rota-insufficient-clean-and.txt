- **Cause insufficiency.** The kitchen is cleaned every day [clean]: knowing the day and being home are not enough; the rota must also assign someone to every day, and each person must be willing to do their turn.
- **Cause insufficiency.** People argue over swapped cleaning days [swaps]: posting a rota alone does not cause arguments; people must also need to swap days, for example because they are away on their assigned day, and there must be no agreed rule for making swaps.
