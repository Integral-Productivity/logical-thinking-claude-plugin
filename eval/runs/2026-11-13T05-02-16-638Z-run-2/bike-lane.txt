- **Entity existence.** Drivers keep out of the marked lane [respect]: paint is no physical barrier, and drivers often park, load, or drift into painted lanes, so this precondition needs its own support (enforcement, a buffer, or evidence of compliance) rather than being assumed.
- **Cause insufficiency.** Cyclists have a marked space on the road [marked]: paint alone produces a usable space only if the main road is wide enough to give a lane without making it too narrow or putting it in the door zone of parked cars.
- **Possible negative branch.** Paint a bike lane on the main road [paint]: narrowing the general traffic lanes can lead drivers to pass cyclists more closely or create congestion, and a painted lane can make cyclists feel safer than they actually are, so the tree should show these undesirable effects.
