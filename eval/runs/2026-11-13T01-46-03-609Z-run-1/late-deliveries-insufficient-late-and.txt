- **Cause insufficiency.** Deliveries arrive on time on peak days [on-time]: enough drivers and route knowledge are not enough, because there must also be enough vehicles for the extra drivers.
- **Cause insufficiency.** Enough drivers work on peak days [enough-drivers]: hiring seasonal drivers gives enough drivers only if enough are recruited and they actually turn up on peak days.
- **Additional cause.** Deliveries are late on peak days [late]: peak-day traffic congestion and parcel volume beyond depot sorting capacity can also make deliveries late, and hiring drivers does not remove those causes.
