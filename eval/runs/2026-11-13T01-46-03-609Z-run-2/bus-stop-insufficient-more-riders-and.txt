- **Cause insufficiency.** More pupils ride the bus [more-riders]: living near a stop is not enough on its own; those pupils must also have been kept off the bus by the distance to a stop, not by a ride, bike, or walk they prefer.
- **Cause insufficiency.** Pupils at the first stop spend longer on the bus [longer-ride]: a longer route only lengthens their ride if the second stop comes after the first stop on the route.
- **Possible negative branch.** More pupils ride the bus [more-riders]: extra riders on an existing bus can overfill it, leaving pupils standing or left behind, and the tree does not draw this.
