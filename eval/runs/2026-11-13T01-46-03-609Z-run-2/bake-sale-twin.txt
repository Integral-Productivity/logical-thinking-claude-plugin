- **Cause insufficiency.** The stall sells muffins [sells]: ready muffins and visitors are not enough on their own, because the visitors must also want to buy muffins at the asking price.
- **Cause insufficiency.** The baker stays up late the night before [late-night]: baking the night before does not by itself mean staying up late, because the baking must also run past the baker's usual bedtime, for example when the evening is otherwise taken up.
