- **Cause insufficiency.** People argue over swapped cleaning days [swaps]: posting a rota does not cause arguments by itself; people must also need to swap days, for example because they are away on their assigned day, and have no agreed way to arrange swaps.
- **Cause insufficiency.** The kitchen is cleaned every day [clean]: knowing the day, having supplies and being home are not enough; people must also be willing to clean on their day, and the rota must assign someone to every day of the week.
