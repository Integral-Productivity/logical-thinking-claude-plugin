- **Cause insufficiency.** More pupils ride the bus [more-riders]: living near a stop is not enough on its own; the bus must also have spare seats, and the pupils must not already prefer another way to school.
- **Cause insufficiency.** More pupils live near a stop [near-stop]: the second stop only helps if it is placed where pupils live who are not already near the first stop.
- **Possible negative branch.** Add a second stop on the school bus route [second-stop]: the extra stop makes the route longer, so pupils ride longer and the bus may reach school later.
