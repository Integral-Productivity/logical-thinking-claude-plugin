- **Cause insufficiency.** The main road loses parking spaces [parking]: painting a lane removes parking only if the road is too narrow to keep both the parking and the new lane.
- **Entity existence.** Drivers keep out of the marked lane [respect]: paint alone does not stop drivers from stopping, loading, or drifting into the lane, so this precondition may not exist without enforcement or physical separation.
- **Possible negative branch.** Paint a bike lane on the main road [paint]: if parking stays next to the lane, cyclists ride in the door zone of parked cars, which the tree does not show.
