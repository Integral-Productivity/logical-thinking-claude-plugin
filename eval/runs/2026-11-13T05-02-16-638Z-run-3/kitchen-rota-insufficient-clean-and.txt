- **Cause insufficiency.** Each person knows their cleaning day [know-day]: posting the rota is not enough; people must also read it and see their own name on a day.
- **Cause insufficiency.** The kitchen is cleaned every day [clean]: knowing the day and being home are not enough; each person must also be willing to clean, and the rota must assign every day to someone.
- **Cause insufficiency.** People argue over swapped cleaning days [swaps]: a posted rota alone does not cause arguments; people must also swap days without a clear, shared agreement on who now has which day.
